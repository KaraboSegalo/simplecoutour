About the brand

We design clothes people actually live in

From quiet studio beginnings to a growing community brand, Simple Couture builds wearable essentials that combine premium fit, local pride, and responsible production.

Our History

Simple Couture started in Johannesburg as a small capsule project focused on elevated basics. As demand grew, we stayed committed to low-volume production and long-lasting craftsmanship.

Mission and Vision

We are shaping a wardrobe culture where quality wins over quantity. Our vision is to make conscious fashion the standard in every South African home.

Sustainability Promise

We prioritize natural fibers, efficient pattern cutting, and reworked seasonal pieces to lower waste. Packaging is recyclable, and suppliers are selected for fair labor and traceable materials.
